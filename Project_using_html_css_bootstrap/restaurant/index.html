Our Story

Nestled in the heart of downtown, La Maison brings the warmth and elegance of French countryside dining to your table. Founded in 2010 by Chef Jean-Luc Moreau, our kitchen blends time-honored traditions with locally sourced ingredients.

Every dish tells a story — from our hand-rolled croissants at dawn to the slow-braised coq au vin that simmers for hours. We believe in food that nourishes both body and soul, served with genuine hospitality in an atmosphere that feels like home.

Join us for lunch, dinner, or a quiet cup of coffee.
